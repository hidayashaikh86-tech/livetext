About Shareli

Our Mission

Shareli was built with a simple goal: to make sharing text, code snippets, and files across devices as fast and frictionless as possible, without ever compromising on security.

In a world where data privacy is increasingly threatened, we believe that moving information from point A to point B should not require creating an account, surrendering personal data, or leaving a permanent digital footprint.

How It Works

When you use Shareli, everything is processed directly in your browser. We utilize the WebCrypto API to encrypt your messages before they ever leave your device. The keys to decrypt this data are contained only in the URL link you share with your recipient. Our servers act merely as a silent relay, completely blind to the contents of your communication.

Built for Everyone

Whether you're a developer needing to securely transfer API keys, a professional sharing a sensitive document, or simply someone wanting to copy a link from their phone to their laptop, Shareli provides the secure ephemeral bridge to get it done instantly.
