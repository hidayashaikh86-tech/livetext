How to Share Text Online (FAQ)

What is Shareli?

Shareli is a free online text sharing platform designed to let you share text securely, simply, and quickly. Whether you need a temporary pastebin, a way to share code snippets, or a secure clipboard between devices, Shareli makes it seamless.

How do I share text with a link?

It's incredibly easy:

  1. Go to the Shareli homepage.
  2. Type or paste your text into the message box.
  3. Click "Send". Your text is instantly encrypted and available in your private live room.
  4. Copy the "Room link" from the sidebar and share it with anyone. They can instantly see the text without refreshing the page!

Is it secure?

Yes. Shareli uses End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) powered by the WebCrypto API. Your messages and files are encrypted directly in your browser. We do not store any of your chat data, logs, or files on our servers. Once you close the room, the data is gone forever.

Can I share files and code?

Absolutely. Shareli supports large file sharing and provides automatic syntax highlighting for code snippets.
