Privacy Policy

Zero Data Retention

Shareli is built on the fundamental principle of absolute privacy. We do not require registration, we do not track your identity, and we do not store your data. All text, code, and files shared through Shareli are ephemeral and exist only while the live room is active.

End-to-End Encryption

All communication within Shareli is protected by state-of-the-art End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) using the WebCrypto API (AES-GCM). The encryption keys are generated in your browser and are never transmitted to or known by our servers. We cannot read your messages or access your files, even if we wanted to.

No Tracking Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics that compromise your privacy.

Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
