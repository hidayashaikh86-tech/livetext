Privacy Policy

Shareli is built around a single privacy principle: your data belongs to you. We do not collect, sell, or store your personal information.

Last updated: August 2026

🔒 Summary: Shareli collects no personal data, uses no tracking cookies, stores no messages permanently, and cannot read your encrypted content. All data self-destructs.

1. What Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. You do not need to provide a name, email address, phone number, or any other personal detail to use Shareli. When you connect to Shareli, the server assigns you a temporary anonymous display name (e.g. "Guest 04") that is discarded when you disconnect.

2. How We Handle Your Messages and Files

All messages and files shared through Shareli are:

  • Encrypted in your browser using AES-GCM 256-bit encryption before being transmitted.
  • Stored only in the server's RAM (temporary memory) — never written to a database or permanent disk storage.
  • Automatically deleted when the self-destruct timer you set expires.
  • Permanently lost when the server restarts — there is no backup or recovery mechanism by design.

The server never has access to the encryption keys used to protect your content. Keys are generated in your browser and exist only in the room URL that you share with your recipients.

3. Cookies and Tracking

Shareli does not use:

  • Tracking cookies or advertising cookies
  • Third-party analytics services (e.g. Google Analytics)
  • Fingerprinting or behavioral tracking
  • Any form of cross-site tracking

The only local storage used is to save your chosen display name between sessions. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared by clearing your browser's local storage.

4. Server Logs

Our hosting provider (Render) may collect basic server access logs (IP address, request timestamps, HTTP status codes) as part of normal infrastructure operation. These logs are managed by Render under their own privacy policy and are not used by Shareli for any purpose.

5. Third-Party Services

Shareli loads fonts from Google Fonts and syntax highlighting libraries from CDN providers. These external requests may result in your IP address being visible to those providers. No personal data is shared with these services by Shareli.

6. Children's Privacy

Shareli is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of Shareli after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
