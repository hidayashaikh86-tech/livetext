How to Share Files Without Google Drive, Dropbox, or Email

Google Drive and Dropbox are great for storing files long-term. But when you just need to send a file to someone right now — quickly and privately — they are completely overkill. Here is the fastest alternative that requires zero accounts and leaves no trace.

Why Google Drive Is Not Always the Right Tool

Google Drive is a powerful cloud storage solution, but it comes with significant trade-offs when you just need to quickly transfer a file:

  • Requires a Google account — the recipient may not have one, or may not want to log in.
  • Permanent storage — uploaded files stay on Google's servers indefinitely unless you manually delete them.
  • Privacy concerns — Google scans files you upload to Drive for various purposes.
  • Slow to set up — uploading, setting share permissions, copying the link, and sending it takes time.

The same problems apply to Dropbox and email attachments. For a quick, one-time transfer, these tools are much heavier than the task requires.

The Fastest Alternative: Shareli

Shareli lets you send any file to any person via a browser — no account on either side, no permanent storage, and the file self-destructs after the timer you set.

  1. Open shareli.online in any browser.
  2. Click "Create Private Room" and share the link with your recipient.
  3. Click the attachment icon in the message bar and select your file.
  4. The file transfers instantly. Images show inline; other files appear as a download button.
  5. Set a 10-minute timer — after that, the file is permanently deleted from the server.
