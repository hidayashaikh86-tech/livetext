Best Free Online Clipboard to Sync Text Across All Your Devices

You copy something on your phone, then realize you need it on your laptop. You copy something on your laptop, then need it on your work desktop. Switching between devices constantly is one of the most annoying daily friction points in modern computing. Here is how to solve it for free.

What is an Online Clipboard?

An online clipboard is a web-based tool that lets you copy content (text, links, code) on one device and instantly access it on a completely different device — without cables, Bluetooth, or accounts. Think of it as a universal paste buffer that lives in the cloud but disappears when you are done.

Why Built-in Sync Solutions Fall Short

Operating systems have started adding their own clipboard sync features, but they all have significant limitations:

  • Apple Universal Clipboard — Works only between Apple devices (iPhone + Mac). Android or Windows users are excluded.
  • Windows Clipboard History (Win+V) — Works only between Windows PCs with the same Microsoft account logged in.
  • Google Gboard sync — Limited, unreliable, and only available on Android.

None of these work if you are mixing operating systems — which most people do. You might have a Windows laptop, an iPhone, and an Android tablet. No native tool handles all three.

Shareli: The Universal Online Clipboard

Shareli works on every device with a browser: iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac, Linux, Chromebook. It does not matter what combination of devices you have. Here is how to use it as an online clipboard:

  1. Open shareli.online in a browser tab on Device A.
  2. Paste or type whatever you want to transfer and press Send.
  3. Open shareli.online in a browser tab on Device B.
  4. Your content appears instantly. Tap to copy it.

Both devices are in the same Public Room by default — so you can do this in under 10 seconds from start to finish.

Keeping It Private

If you are transferring sensitive content like a password or private note, use a Private Room instead. Create a private room on Device A, then open the same room URL on Device B. Everything in a private room is end-to-end encrypted — no one else can see it, and it disappears on schedule.

Use It as a Permanent Tab

Many Shareli users keep a browser tab open permanently on both their laptop and phone. Whenever they need to cross something between devices, they just paste it into Shareli and grab it on the other side. It becomes a seamless part of the workflow once it is set up.

You can even install Shareli as a PWA (Progressive Web App) by tapping "Add to Home Screen" on your phone, giving it an app icon for instant access.

Unlike tools like Google Keep or Apple Notes, Shareli does not require an account. Nothing you paste is stored permanently. Set a 10-minute timer and your clipboard content disappears the moment you no longer need it. This is the most privacy-respecting clipboard solution available.
