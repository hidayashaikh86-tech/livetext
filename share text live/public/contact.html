What can you contact us about?

  • 🐛 Bug Reports — Found something broken? Tell us what happened and which browser or device you were using.
  • 💡 Feature Requests — Have an idea that would make Shareli better? We are always open to new suggestions.
  • 🔒 Security Issues — Found a potential vulnerability? Please report it responsibly via email rather than publicly disclosing it.
  • 🚫 Abuse Reports — If you believe Shareli is being used for illegal activity, contact us immediately with the room link and we will terminate the session.
  • 💬 General Feedback — We appreciate all feedback, positive or critical. It helps us improve the product.

A Note on Abuse Reports

Shareli uses end-to-end encryption, which means we cannot read the contents of any room. However, we can immediately terminate any session if provided with the room URL and evidence of misuse. We take abuse reports very seriously and aim to respond within 24 hours.
